The General tab in the Audience section of Display & Video 360 (DV360) serves as your Library of 1st-party audiences. Website & App Visitors (Tag-based), Customer Match Lists, Analytics-Based Audiences.
Custom Lists
This option lets you build tailored audiences based on specific user intent or broad interests using custom parameters:
• Custom Affinity: Reach audiences based on their habits, specific interests, or media consumption patterns (defined by entering specific keywords, URLs, or mobile apps).
• Custom Intent: Target users actively researching or intending to buy specific products and services. For YouTube campaigns, this includes targeting users based on the specific search terms they have entered on Google.
Combined
This option allows you to merge multiple distinct audience lists into a single segment using AND, OR, and NOT Boolean logic:
• Cross-Category Layering: Combine your 1st-party site visitors (from the General tab) with Google’s In-Market or Affinity audiences and custom lists.
• Exclusion Logic: Narrow down target reach by including specific high-intent segments while explicitly excluding existing converters or specific custom lists.
